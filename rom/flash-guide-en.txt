##### Device boot #####
1.Please install the usb driver for the first flashing, double-click to run adb-setup-1.4.4.exe, and then enter Y for all options until the installation is complete
2.Please do not flash the device when the device is low in power, and please keep more than 40% of the device's power
3.In the boot state, run the script 920_fastboot_all.bat, the device will automatically restart and enter the flashing mode
4.Wait for the script to show the "fastboot success" field, indicating that the flashing is successful, and the device will automatically restart and enter the system
